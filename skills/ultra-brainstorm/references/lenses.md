# Lenses for ultra-brainstorm idea lanes

Lane `i` takes lens `i mod 32` and seed `i`, so neighbouring lanes never share a lens and a run of 100
lanes uses every lens at least three times with different seeds. Keep the list at 32 or more. Each line is a
question one lane can act on with nothing but the topic brief.

1. Inversion: what would the opposite aim look like, and which part of it is right?
2. Drop a constraint: remove one real limit; what opens up, and what still holds once it is back?
3. Borrowed mechanism: which unrelated field solved a problem of this shape, and what can be lifted from it?
4. The extreme user: the most demanding, the newest, or the most hurried person.
5. Take away: what can be removed so the rest works better?
6. Odd pair: join two existing things that are never joined.
7. Another decade: how was this done ten years ago, and how will it be done ten years from now?
8. Swap the scarce thing: replace the resource that is short with one that is plentiful.
9. Flip the worst idea: write the worst answer, then reverse its worst trait.
10. Better question: answer the question that should have been asked.
11. By tomorrow: what works by tomorrow for almost no cost?
12. Ten times: what would make this ten times better, whatever it costs?
13. Do nothing: what happens if nothing is done, and when is that the best answer?
14. Runs by itself: what could happen without anyone deciding each time?
15. By hand first: what would a person do with paper and patience before any tool exists?
16. The default: which default, if changed, changes everyone's behaviour?
17. Safe failure: how does this fail, and what design makes the failure harmless?
18. Instant yes: what would make a careful person agree at once?
19. Count it: what can be measured, and which idea follows from measuring it?
20. Close the loop: where can the result feed the next attempt?
21. Neighbours: who else touches this, and what would they build on top of it?
22. One person, one day: the smallest version that still helps.
23. A thousand of them: what changes at a thousand users or a thousand items?
24. Another medium: text, voice, picture, sound, motion, or a physical object.
25. Another place: a terminal, a phone, paper, a meeting room, a car.
26. Who gains: who is rewarded today, and what if someone else were?
27. Reorder: change the order of the steps; what improves?
28. All at once or a trickle: one batch, or a continuous stream of small pieces?
29. Borrowed ritual: a checklist, a ceremony or a habit from another craft.
30. Teach it: what would make this easy to explain to a newcomer?
31. No expert in the room: what if nobody involved knew the field?
32. Contrarian: which belief everyone here holds is probably wrong?
