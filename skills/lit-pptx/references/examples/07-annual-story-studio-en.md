# Neighbourhood reading rooms 2026
---
layout: asymmetric-feature

## The network at a glance

![Evening study table at the riverside room](assets/example-b.png)

- **Rooms** 23 open, up from 17 in 2025, each within 800 m of a primary school
- **Hours** 61 hours a week on average, with four rooms open until 22:00
- **Volunteers** 412 active, giving 38,600 hours across the year
- **Cost** 1.9M EUR running cost, 82 EUR per registered reader
- Source: Example Reading Rooms Trust annual accounts, Sep 2025-Aug 2026

## Evening hours trial, four rooms

:::: columns 1fr 1fr
::: col
- **Before the trial**
  (1) Hours: closing at 19:00 on weekdays
  (2) Visits: 11,200 a month across the four rooms
  (3) Users: 22% aged 16-24
  (4) Cost: 6,100 EUR a month for staff
:::
::: col
- **During the trial**
  (1) Hours: closing at 22:00 on weekdays
  (2) Visits: 15,900 a month across the four rooms
  (3) Users: 37% aged 16-24
  (4) Cost: 8,400 EUR a month for staff
:::
::::

- Source: trial report, March-August 2026; staff cost from payroll
